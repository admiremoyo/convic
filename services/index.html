What we do

Four trades, one accountable contractor

Electrical, solar, water and plumbing delivered by a single team — so the conduit, the pipework and the pump are coordinated instead of contested.

Electrical Installations

New installations, rewires and maintenance for homes, offices, factories and farms — from first-fix conduit set into the slab through to distribution boards, testing and handover. Including 11kV and medium-voltage overhead lines, transformer connections and site reticulation.

  • Domestic & commercial wiring and rewires
  • Industrial power, control panels & motor control
  • 11kV & medium-voltage lines
  • Lighting design and installation
  • Fault finding & planned maintenance

Explore electrical services

Solar & Battery Storage

Grid-tied, hybrid and off-grid systems sized from your actual consumption rather than a shelf package. Lithium storage, proper DC and AC protection, and essential-load boards so the system carries what matters when the grid drops.

  • Hybrid & off-grid inverters
  • Lithium (LiFePO₄) battery banks
  • Panels, mounting frames & protection kits
  • Solar borehole pumping
  • Solar geysers & system servicing

Explore solar services

Boreholes & Water Systems

The whole chain from borehole to tap — pump sized against yield and head, storage that carries you through an outage, and a pressure system that delivers a proper shower on the first floor.

  • Borehole pump installation & repair
  • Reservoir and storage tanks
  • Pressure tanks & booster pumps
  • Solar borehole pumping
  • Irrigation & water reticulation

Explore water systems

Plumbing & Drainage

New plumbing installations and drainage systems with correct falls, accessible isolation and enough inspection points to clear a blockage without excavating the garden — plus the maintenance that prevents the emergency.

  • Hot & cold water installations
  • Drainage, sewer & storm water
  • Septic tanks & soakaways
  • Geysers & sanitaryware
  • Leak detection & repairs

How a job runs

From first call to final handover

The same five steps whether it is a leaking geyser or a factory reticulation.

1

Call or WhatsApp

Tell us what you need. We will say straight away whether it is something we do and roughly when we can get to it.

2

Site assessment

We come and look properly — loads, distances, water pressure, roof access. Free within Harare.

3

Itemised quote

Equipment, labour, timeline and exclusions in writing, so you can compare it against anyone else's.

4

Installation

Our own team on site, working to the agreed programme and keeping you updated as it progresses.

5

Test & hand over

Everything tested and commissioned, boards labelled, and a walkthrough so you know how your system works.
