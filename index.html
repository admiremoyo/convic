Load shedding, solved properly

Stop rationing your day around the ZESA schedule

A solar system is not a generator you switch on when the lights go. Done properly it is a second supply that carries your house or your production line without anyone noticing the grid has dropped — and it quietly reduces the bill on the days the grid is up.

The difference between a system that works for ten years and one that disappoints in eighteen months is almost never the panels. It is the sizing, the cable, the protection and the workmanship behind the board.

  • Sized on your actual load — we audit what you run and for how long before we quote a single panel.
  • Lithium (LiFePO₄) storage — deeper usable capacity and far longer cycle life than lead-acid.
  • Proper DC & AC protection — isolators, breakers and surge arrestors fitted as standard, not as an upsell.
  • Grid-tied, hybrid or fully off-grid — including solar borehole pumping and solar geysers.

The boring things done right

Anyone can hang a panel or pull a cable. What separates a good installation from an expensive lesson is what happens where you cannot see it.

We start before the slab

Conduit, sleeves and drainage set into the reinforcement before the concrete goes down. Get that wrong and you are chasing walls and breaking floors later — at your cost, not ours.

One contract, one programme

Electrical, plumbing, borehole and solar coordinated by one team. No waiting on a subcontractor who is on another site, and no arguments about whose pipe crossed whose conduit.

Quoted honestly, up front

An itemised quote showing equipment, labour and what is excluded. If a job needs more than you first asked for, you hear it before we start — not as a surprise at the end.

Equipment we will stand behind

Inverters, batteries, pumps and protection gear we have installed repeatedly and can service. We would rather lose a quote than fit something we know fails in year two.

Tested and handed over

Circuits tested, boards labelled, systems commissioned and the client shown how to operate them. You should not need to phone us to reset your own inverter.

We answer the phone afterwards

Maintenance and callbacks are part of the job. The number you called to get the quote is the same number that picks up when something needs attention two years later.

Questions we get asked

Straight answers before you commit

Which areas of Harare do you cover?

We are based at No. 20 Lower Hampden Place in Marlborough and work right across Harare — Borrowdale, Mount Pleasant, Avondale, Greendale, Highlands, Glen Lorne, Belvedere, Waterfalls, Chitungwiza, Norton, Ruwa and the surrounding farming areas. For larger commercial, industrial and medium-voltage contracts we travel to sites countrywide.

How much does a solar system cost in Zimbabwe?

The price depends on what you need to run and for how long. A small backup system covering lights, Wi-Fi, TV and a fridge costs far less than a system that carries a borehole pump, geyser and air conditioning. We size every system from your actual consumption rather than selling a fixed package, so the honest answer comes after a site assessment — which is free within Harare.

Do you install both on-grid and off-grid solar systems?

Yes. We install grid-tied and hybrid systems that work alongside your ZESA supply and cut your monthly bill, as well as fully off-grid systems with lithium battery storage for properties with no reliable connection. We also supply and fit the DC and AC protection kits, surge arrestors and isolators that keep the installation safe and the warranty valid.

Can you work on 11kV and medium-voltage lines?

Yes. Alongside domestic and commercial work we install and maintain 11kV and medium-voltage overhead lines, transformer connections and reticulation for farms, mines, industrial premises and new developments.

Do you handle both the electrical and the plumbing on a new build?

That is exactly what we are set up for. Running the electrical first fix, the plumbing and drainage, the borehole and the solar through one contractor means the conduit, pipework and slab work are coordinated on site instead of three trades blaming each other. You get one programme, one point of contact and one quote.

How quickly can you come out for a fault?

Call or WhatsApp 077 227 5744 and we will tell you honestly when we can be there. Faults that leave a property without power or water, or that present a safety risk, go to the front of the queue.
