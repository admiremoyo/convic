About us

A contractor you can hold responsible for the whole job

Covcro Electrical & Plumbing (Pvt) Ltd is a registered Zimbabwean company based in Marlborough, Harare.

Who we are

Built around the trades that keep buildings working

Covcro exists because of a problem every property owner in Harare recognises. The electrician finishes and leaves. The plumber arrives and finds the conduit where the waste pipe was supposed to go. The solar installer turns up two weeks later and wants the board rewired. Everyone is confident the problem belongs to somebody else, and the client pays for all three visits.

We carry electrical, solar, water and plumbing in one company so that conversation never happens. One team plans the services together, installs them in the right order, and answers for the result.

We work for homeowners, developers, builders, farms, businesses and industrial clients — anything from a tripping distribution board to an 11kV line extension and a full site reticulation.

How we work

The standards we hold ourselves to

None of these are remarkable. They are simply the things that get skipped when a job is being done cheaply.

Quote before we start

An itemised quote covering equipment, labour and exclusions. If the scope changes, you hear about it before the work happens — not on the final invoice.

Protection is standard

Isolators, breakers, surge protection, dry-run protection, drip trays and safety valves are part of the installation, not an optional line item to be negotiated away.

Sized, not guessed

Inverters sized on surge as well as running load. Pumps matched to borehole yield and head. Cable sized for the distance it actually runs.

Tested and labelled

Circuits tested, boards labelled, systems commissioned, and a walkthrough so you can operate your own installation without phoning us.

Equipment we can service

We fit inverters, batteries, pumps and gear we have installed before and can get parts for. Cheap unknown equipment is somebody else's problem in year two — usually yours.

Reachable afterwards

The number that answered when you asked for a quote is the number that answers when something needs attention later. Maintenance is part of the job, not an inconvenience.

Who we work for

Homeowners

Rewires, board upgrades, lighting, solar backup, borehole and pressure systems, geysers, drainage and the repairs in between. Most of our residential work is in Harare's northern and eastern suburbs, but we cover the whole city.

Builders and developers

Full electrical and plumbing packages on new builds, coordinated with your programme. Getting the first fix into the slab on time is the single biggest thing a services contractor can do for a build, and it is where we put our attention.

Businesses and industry

Three-phase distribution, control panels, machine connections, warehouse lighting, emergency lighting, commercial plumbing and planned maintenance — scheduled around production rather than in spite of it.

Farms and estates

11kV and medium-voltage line work, transformer connections, site reticulation, borehole pumping, storage, irrigation mainlines and drip systems. Off-grid solar where the network does not reach or cannot be relied on.
