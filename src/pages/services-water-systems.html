A borehole is only half a water supply. What turns it into running water is everything above it — the pump matched to the depth and yield, the storage that carries you through an outage, and the pressure system that makes a shower on the first floor behave like a shower rather than a dribble. Covcro installs and maintains that whole chain.

Borehole pump installation

Pump selection is where most water systems are won or lost. Too small and it never fills the tank; too large and it draws the borehole down, runs dry, and burns out. The right choice comes from the borehole's depth, static water level and yield, plus the head and distance it has to push against.

  • Submersible pump supply, installation and setting to depth
  • Pump sizing against borehole yield, head and daily demand
  • Rising main, cable, safety rope and headworks
  • Control boxes, dry-run protection and level control
  • Pump removal, testing, repair and replacement
  • Existing installation assessment and fault finding

Dry-run protection matters. The most common cause of a dead borehole pump in Harare is running it against an empty borehole. Level or current-based protection costs a fraction of a replacement pump and we fit it as standard.

Reservoir and storage tanks

Storage is what makes a water supply resilient. It lets the pump work in short, efficient runs, carries you through a power outage or a dry borehole recovery period, and — when it is elevated — delivers water by gravity without a pump running at all.

  • Reservoir and storage tank supply and installation
  • Tank stands and elevated platforms
  • Multiple-tank manifolds, balancing and isolation
  • Inlet, outlet, overflow, drain and float-valve assemblies
  • Municipal backup storage and automatic switchover
  • Tank cleaning, repair and re-plumbing

Pressure tanks and booster pumps

Gravity from a low tank stand gives you flow but not much pressure. A pressure tank and pump set gives you consistent pressure at every outlet — and the pressure vessel is what stops the pump switching on and off every few seconds, which is what destroys pumps and pressure switches.

  • Pressure tank and pump set sizing and installation
  • Booster pumps for multi-storey and long-run reticulation
  • Variable-speed pumps for constant pressure
  • Pressure switch setting, bladder charging and commissioning
  • Non-return valves, strainers and isolation
  • Diagnosis of short cycling, pressure loss and pump chatter

Solar borehole pumping

Where there is no reliable grid — or where you simply do not want the pump on your electricity bill — a solar array can drive the pump directly through the day and fill elevated storage. No batteries, no fuel, and the tank does the work at night. It is one of the most economically sensible solar applications available in Zimbabwe.

We size the array against the pump's requirement and the tank against your daily demand, so the system fills reliably even on an overcast day. See solar and battery storage for the electrical side.

Water system FAQs

Borehole and pump questions

Do you drill boreholes as well?

Our work starts once the hole is there. We install, commission and maintain the pump, rising main, storage, pressure system and reticulation — and we are happy to advise on what your drilled borehole's depth and yield mean for the equipment it needs.

My pump runs but the tank never fills. What is wrong?

Usually one of four things: the borehole yield is lower than the pump's draw so it is running dry, the foot or non-return valve is failing and letting water fall back, there is a leak in the rising main, or the pump itself has worn impellers. We test rather than guess — and we will tell you if the answer is a smaller pump rather than a bigger one.

Why does my pressure pump switch on and off constantly?

That is short cycling, and it is almost always a pressure tank that has lost its air charge or has a failed bladder. It destroys pumps and pressure switches quickly. Recharging or replacing the vessel and resetting the switch normally solves it.

Can I run my borehole pump on solar?

Yes, and it is often the best value solar you can buy. A dedicated array drives the pump through the day and fills elevated storage, so you get water by gravity at night without any battery at all. Where you already have a hybrid system, we can also run the pump off it — but pumping into storage is usually cheaper.

Do you install irrigation for farms and greenhouses?

Yes — mainlines, manifolds, valve assemblies, zone control and drip lines for field crops, greenhouses, nurseries and estates, fed from borehole and storage.
