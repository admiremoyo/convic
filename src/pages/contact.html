Before you call

Things that help us quote faster

For solar enquiries

A recent electricity bill and a rough list of what you need to keep running during an outage — and for how long. If you know your roof orientation or have a photo of it, even better.

For borehole and pump enquiries

The borehole depth, static water level and yield if you have the drilling report, plus what is currently installed and what it is doing (or not doing). A photo of the existing headworks and control box helps a lot.

For electrical faults

What trips, how often, and whether it is one circuit or the main. A photo of the distribution board with the cover off tells us most of what we need.

For new builds

Plans if you have them, the stage the build is at, and your builder's programme. If the slab has not been poured yet, get us in early — that is when the first fix has to happen.
